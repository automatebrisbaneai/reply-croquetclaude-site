Thank you John, Welcome to your new position as President of CAQ. I am very active in my role as coach and referee in all 4 codes of croquet. I have recently been trained, along with other QLD's, to be a person who can train coaches. I carry out these roles mainly on the Gold Coast and to a lesser extent in Brisbane. I prefer not to attend meetings but to network with relevant people in these roles to organise my activities. I would like to see the regions take on a greater responsibility for organising coaching and maintaining the updates for referees, along with regional tournaments and pennants. I feel that it would be beneficial to you to have one spokes person per region to report to you and to pass on questions that the regional person is unable to answer. If you would like more suggestions on why's to encourage clubs to increase and encourage for players to improve I am happy to offer my suggestions. So in the background I become your support person, not directly communicating with you. Regards, Kathie Grant Southport Croquet Club
