01 — Who solves what

We can solve anything that can be defined.

Most problems involve two kinds of expert.

Domain experts define the problem. The systems expert solves it. The communication between the two is the issue.

Defining the problem belongs to the domain expert. Without that piece, the systems expert has nothing to build.

02 — Why we've been stuck

The bit we've struggled with is #1: the definition.

CAQ and I have struggled because of issue #1, which is the definition of the problem. I've tried to define problems myself and I've tried waiting for the definition to be given to me. Neither has jived.

So with that framing in mind, the answer is: define what you want, and I'll #2 it (implement).

Where my value comes in is I have strong croquet knowledge as well, so you can ask me about handicaps in both senses, croquet and systems.

When it's said we need a croquet person to be Operating Officer, what it's really saying is: we can't define our problems and we need someone who understands the domain and the systems to define the problem we can't, and put it together.

Rejecting a definition without replacing it with a new one leaves the systems expert with nothing to solve.

05 — The maths

Looking at the (non-world-ranking) handicap system mathematically: it does nothing.

1

Vast majority of players relying on the system are in Division 3. Divisions have little use in practice. You can see this in different regions defining their divisions differently.

2

Variance in play, rather than difference in handicap, is what decides outcomes. The handicap cluster shows most people have their middle-of-the-road level and just vary around that. Their game could deviate by 6 points (play like a 5 or an 11 in any single game), but over their whole career they'll be lucky to move 2 points.

A whole lot of effort is done to maintain a system that mathematically has no measurable conclusions that can be drawn. The variance is too high and sample sizes too low in the middle of the curve.

When variance drops and game numbers rise, we have the ELO-based world rankings.
